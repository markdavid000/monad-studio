# How the app looks and talks, in plain English

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Technical version:** [ADR-2026-11-11-design-and-copy.md](ADR-2026-11-11-design-and-copy.md)

## What is the problem?

The current site looks like many AI-made sites: glowing purple gradients, five bright colours, identical cards and big promises. Some of what it says is not true, like "Connected" when nothing is checked, and links to pages that do not exist.

## What are we doing about it?

A calm, plain look like the tools developers already trust: neutral colours, one accent colour, clear text, and a real example of the product on the front page instead of decoration. All the wording lives in one place per tool and is checked automatically for hype words and claims we cannot back up. Light and dark mode both work.

## What changes for people?

Visitors see what the product does in the first screen. The site loads faster because it uses fewer fonts and less animation.

## What could go wrong?

A plain design can feel empty. The real product example on the landing page carries the page; the owner reviews it before it ships.
